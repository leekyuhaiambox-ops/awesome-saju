## Each pillar's reading includes

- Personality archetype with strengths and tensions
- Career fit
- Romantic compatibility patterns
- Energy stage (one of the 12 life stages)
- Hidden stems within the earthly branch

## Why 60?

10 heavenly stems × 12 earthly branches = 120 combinations, but only 60 are valid because the stem and branch must share the same yin-yang polarity. This 60-cycle is called the **sexagenary cycle** (六十甲子, yukcipgapja).

Used to organize East Asian calendars for over 2,000 years.
