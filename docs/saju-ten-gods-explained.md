# Ten Gods (십신) in Korean Saju — Plain Explanation

The Ten Gods are 10 *relational roles* that other characters in your chart play toward your **day master** (the heavenly stem of your day pillar).

## The 10 Gods

Each god is defined by two attributes:
1. **Same element vs different element** compared to your day master
2. **Same polarity** (both yang or both yin) **vs different polarity**

| God | Korean | Element relation | Polarity | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 비견 | Bigyeon | Same element | Same polarity | Sibling, peer, competitor |
| 겁재 | Geopjae | Same element | Different polarity | Rival, friend who takes |
| 식신 | Siksin | Day master generates | Same polarity | Creative output, calm pursuits |
| 상관 | Sanggwan | Day master generates | Different polarity | Bold expression, criticism |
| 정재 | Jeongjae | Day master controls | Different polarity | Stable income, traditional wealth |
| 편재 | Pyeonjae | Day master controls | Same polarity | Flexible wealth, opportunities |
| 정관 | Jeonggwan | Controls day master | Different polarity | Authority, formal recognition |
| 편관 | Pyeongwan | Controls day master | Same polarity | Pressure, challenge, transformation |
| 정인 | Jeongin | Generates day master | Different polarity | Mother, formal learning, support |
| 편인 | Pyeonin | Generates day master | Same polarity | Step-mother, niche knowledge |

## How to read your chart

Look at the year pillar, month pillar, hour pillar — each contains stems and branches. For each, determine the god relative to your day master.
